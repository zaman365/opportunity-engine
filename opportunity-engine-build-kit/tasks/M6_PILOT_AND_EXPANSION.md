# M6 · operational pilot and controlled expansion

## Outcome

The system completes useful, profitable work with credible evidence and manageable delivery capacity. Only then add another venture adapter or agency-facing SaaS scope.

## Tasks

Complete security/accessibility/restore/release runbooks; owner-approved production rollout with limited tenants and caps; instrument review time, false positives/abstention, cost, paid acceptance, labor/rework, refunds and collected contribution. Capture rejected candidates for selection-bias review. Do not infer success from report count.

Validate one new adapter (LokalFix or MikroIT) with its own evidence rubric, permission/source policy, catalog, delivery owner and test examples. Do not clone PDP scoring onto restaurant/IT workflows. Document separate legal controllers and client-data boundaries.

## Gate

All production acceptance blockers resolved; restore and revocation exercised; no unhandled cap/permission incidents; paid work meets agreed contribution floor; queue fits capacity; multiple independent customers validate the offer. Pricing and 90-day forecasts in original blueprint remain hypotheses until measured.

External SaaS, mass data licensing, automatic changes and new marketplaces require separate scoped decisions; they are not automatic “final phase” checkboxes.
