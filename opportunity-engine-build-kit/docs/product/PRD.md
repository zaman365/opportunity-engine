# Product requirements

## Mandate

Enable a small expert team to turn supported observations into profitable completed work. Customer trust is earned by showing what was inspected, what was found, what remains unknown and what the proposed service includes.

## Users and jobs

| User | Job | Successful outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | Select a permitted account and run a bounded check | Scan accepted with real state and visible cost cap |
| Reviewer | Decide whether an observation supports a customer-facing finding | Evidence and contrary evidence reviewed without context hunting |
| Delivery lead | Accept a scope the team can actually deliver | Versioned deliverable, prerequisites, effort and acceptance tests |
| Owner | Control membership, resources, policy and capacity | Explicit approvals, audit trail, cost and queue visibility |
| Customer, from M3 | Request a useful check and buy a defined improvement | Honest report, private access, clear scope and status |

## Core objects and language

An **account** is a business; an **asset** is a page or authorized resource; a **scan** is one bounded inspection; **evidence** is captured observation; a **finding** is a reviewable assertion; an **opportunity** joins supported need to an eligible service; an **engagement** is accepted work. Do not call every account a lead or every hypothesis an issue.

## M1 scope

Internal operator only. Explicitly approved accounts/targets; MF-LINK-01; up to three candidates selected under the scan's page ceiling; two clean-session checks of an important link; human review; private report. All money is internal cost accounting, not automatic customer charging. At least two isolated test tenants. English operator text; German and English customer report templates are specified for later use.

## Requirements by journey

Intake explains sample and cap before confirmation. Return an actual job ID only after durable admission. Status presents step names, captured denominator and partial reasons, never imaginary percent completion. Detail preserves capture conditions. A reviewer cannot confirm missing evidence or a stale version. Report generation consumes an immutable reviewed snapshot, not mutable live draft rows. A declined finding remains auditable. Empty reports clearly state no supported finding in the sample, not universal health.

## Discovery and routing

M1 is not a prospect scraper. Company discovery, partner imports and authorized connectors arrive behind separate adapters and source policies. A new venture requires detector rubric, eligible catalog scope, legal controller, permissions and delivery owner. Never activate a generic venture tab solely because its name appears in the blueprint.

## Outcomes and non-goals

Measure actual review time, useful accepted findings, safe completion rate, delivery effort and collected contribution. Defer contact warehouses, mass outreach, AI-generated product photography, generalized workflow builders, invented revenue scores, payment automation and autonomous remediation.
