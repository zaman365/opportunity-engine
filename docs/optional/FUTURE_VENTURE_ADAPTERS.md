# Optional · Future venture adapters

**Not implemented. Not in any current milestone.**

## What they would be

Vertical adapters — LokalFix, MikroIT, restaurants, IT services and so on — each with its own
ideal-customer definition, its own detectors, its own offer catalogue and its own delivery
owner.

## Why none of them is being built

The handoff's own expansion rule is the right one, and it is not met by any of these yet:

> add another venture only when its adapter can specify credible evidence, an eligible offer,
> a permitted acquisition path and a delivery owner.

A vertical adapter with no delivery owner is a landing page. One with no permitted acquisition
path is worse than that: for German B2B, the obvious path — cold email — is unlawful (see
[the DACH study](../legal/DACH_OUTREACH_STUDY.md)), so "we will find customers somehow" is not
a plan, it is the hardest part left unstated.

The deeper reason is about what the engine is for. The Consistency Engine is shared
infrastructure, and that is a genuine benefit. Shared _unproven assumptions_ are not. Every
adapter added before one is proven multiplies the cost of discovering the assumption was
wrong.

## What would have to be true first

1. The first vertical has paying pilot customers and a repeatable delivery.
2. The proposed adapter names a detector that produces evidence a buyer in that vertical
   recognises as a problem.
3. It names a lawful way to reach those buyers, in the jurisdiction they are in.
4. It names the person who delivers the work.

## What a venture adapter must never be

A reason to weaken a detector. If a vertical needs a claim the evidence does not support, the
answer is no adapter, not a looser rule.
